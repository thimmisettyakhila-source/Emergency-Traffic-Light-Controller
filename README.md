Problem Statement:

In many cities, traffic lights operate on fixed timing and do not respond instantly to emergency vehicles.
This causes ambulances to wait at signals, leading to critical delays during medical emergencies.

Proposed Solution:

A smart traffic signal controller that:

Detects emergency vehicles from any direction

Overrides normal traffic operation

Provides instant green signal to the emergency direction

Turns all other directions red

Safely resumes normal operation after emergency clearance
